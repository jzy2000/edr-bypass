# Evidence

The oracle is the product's own reaction: a block, a log line, an alert, or the sensor still enforcing. A custom probe that says the byte was written is not a miss until the product has had time to react.

Record privilege, the single variable you changed, and hold, miss, or inconclusive. Inconclusive means the feature was off, the oracle was down, or the token was exempt (SYSTEM, own image, or parent of a create).

A bugcheck is recorded from a full memory dump: bugcheck code, faulting instruction, and the product driver frames. Do not cite a minidump.
