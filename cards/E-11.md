# E-11

Test whether LOLBAS delivery bypasses content detection.

1. Create a payload the product detects when written directly (baseline).
2. Encode it: `certutil -encode payload.exe payload.b64`.
3. Decode via LOLBAS: `certutil -decode payload.b64 payload_decoded.exe`.
4. Also try: `expand payload.cab -F:* C:\TEMP\` (pack first with `makecab`).
5. Also try: `bitsadmin /transfer job /download http://localhost/payload.exe C:\TEMP\bits.exe`.
6. If the product detects all delivery methods, record `hold`. If any evade, record `miss`.
