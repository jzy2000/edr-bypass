# B-03

Test service stop and recovery.

1. `sc stop <service>`. Record whether it succeeds.
2. If it succeeds, measure time to auto-restart (the driver or a watchdog should restart it).
3. Before restart, attempt to wipe FailureActions: `sc failure <service> reset= 0 actions= ///`.
4. Stop again. If FailureActions was wiped and the service does not restart, record `miss`.
5. Attempt `sc delete <service>`. Record.
6. If the driver or a watchdog restarts the service and FailureActions cannot be wiped, record `hold`.
7. Restore registry. Restart the service.
