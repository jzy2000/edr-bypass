# F-09

Test volume-level bypass of the minifilter.

1. Open the volume: `CreateFileW("\\\\.\\C:", GENERIC_READ | GENERIC_WRITE, FILE_SHARE_READ | FILE_SHARE_WRITE, ...)`.
2. Attempt a raw read at the file's disk offset (requires knowledge of NTFS layout).
3. Attempt `FSCTL_LOCK_VOLUME` followed by `FSCTL_DISMOUNT_VOLUME` on the volume containing `install_dir`.
4. If the dismount succeeds, remount and check if the minifilter re-attaches: `fltmc instances`.
5. If the filter is gone after remount, attempt to modify protected files. Record `miss`.
6. If raw access is blocked or the filter re-attaches, record `hold`.
7. Remount the volume. Restore from snapshot.
