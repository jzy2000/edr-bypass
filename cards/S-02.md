# S-02

Record the product's component versions and operating state.

1. **Agent version**: `(Get-Item "$env:install_dir\*.exe").VersionInfo | Select FileName,FileVersion`.
2. **Driver version**: `fltmc filters` to find the minifilter name. Then `(Get-Item "$env:SystemRoot\System32\drivers\<driver>.sys").VersionInfo.FileVersion`.
3. **Companion DLL version**: if the product injects a DLL into processes, find it: `tasklist /m | findstr /i <dllname>`. Get its version: `(Get-Item "$env:SystemRoot\System32\<dll>").VersionInfo.FileVersion`.
4. **OS build**: `[System.Environment]::OSVersion.Version` and `(Get-ItemProperty "HKLM:\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows NT\CurrentVersion").DisplayVersion`.
5. **Policy mode**: check the product log or registry for whether anti-tamper/detection policies are active vs off/default.
6. **Cloud connectivity**: check the product log for recent heartbeat or sync entries. If the last sync was >5 minutes ago, the agent may be disconnected.
7. Record all findings. No cleanup needed.
