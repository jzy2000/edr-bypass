# F-10

Test whether Safe Mode bypasses file protection.

1. Enable Safe Mode: `bcdedit /set {current} safeboot minimal`.
2. Reboot into Safe Mode.
3. In Safe Mode, the minifilter driver likely does not load (not marked as boot-critical, or safe-mode-aware).
4. Attempt: `del "%install_dir%\<agent_exe>"`. Record success/error.
5. Attempt: `ren "%install_dir%\<driver>.sys" renamed.bak`. Record.
6. Attempt: `del "%data_dir%\*.*" /Q`. Record.
7. Disable Safe Mode: `bcdedit /deletevalue {current} safeboot`.
8. Reboot to normal mode.
9. If the product repairs replaced files and alerts on tampering, record `hold`.
10. If the files remain deleted/renamed and the product fails to start, record `miss`.
11. Restore from snapshot.
