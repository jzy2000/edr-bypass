# S-04

Record which tokens are exempt from the product's self-protect before testing.

1. From Session 1 admin, run: `whoami /all` and record SID, groups, integrity level.
2. Identify SYSTEM: `sc query` the product service. Note the logon account. If the product runs as SYSTEM, that token bypasses many "own process or system" gates. Do not use WinRM or PsExec-as-SYSTEM as the primary attacker.
3. Identify the product's own image path: `(Get-Process -Name <agent_process> -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue).Path`. Any caller whose image matches may receive full access from ObRegisterCallbacks.
4. Note: a parent of a `CreateProcess` on the product binary gets a full parent handle. That is an exemption, not a bypass, unless the parent is not the product.
