# A-12

Gate: crash evidence quality.

For every crash finding:
1. Is the evidence from `C:\Windows\MEMORY.DMP` (full dump)?
2. Does the verdict include: bugcheck code, faulting instruction (address + disassembly), and the product driver frames on the stack?
3. If the evidence is from a minidump or is incomplete, re-capture with a full dump.
4. Do not reference minidumps in findings.
