Gate: feature-on verification.

For every result:
1. Was the feature on? Check the product log for policy application.
2. Was the agent connected to the cloud? An unconnected agent may be in default/off mode.
3. If the feature was off during the test, change the verdict to `inconclusive`.
4. Re-run with the feature confirmed on.
