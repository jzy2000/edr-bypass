# B-04

Test the timing window between service stop and restart.

1. `sc stop <service>`.
2. Immediately attempt to replace the agent binary: `copy /Y C:\TEMP\test.exe "%install_dir%\<agent_exe>"`.
3. Wait for the auto-restart (from FailureActions or the driver watchdog).
4. If the replaced binary is loaded, record `miss`.
5. If the driver keeps the files locked during the restart window, or re-verifies on start, record `hold`.
6. Restore from snapshot.
