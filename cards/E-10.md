# E-10

Test whether forged ETW events can command the driver.

1. Identify ETW providers the product consumes (from the driver binary or ETW session enumeration).
2. Write events mimicking trusted commands:
   a. "Terminate process PID=X"
   b. "Add exclusion path=C:\"
   c. "Disable sensor"
3. If the driver acts on the forged event, record `miss`.
4. If the driver ignores non-authenticated events, record `hold`.
